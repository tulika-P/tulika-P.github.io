Platform Foundations

TikTok is not Instagram with shorter videos. It runs on completely different social logic — entertainment-first, algorithm-driven, and structured around communities rather than followers.

Lessons

1.1
What makes TikTok structurally different from every other platformThe For You Page vs. follower feeds — why a brand-new account can go viral on day one, and why 10K followers mean almost nothing here.
1.2
The TikTok audience in 2025Demographics have expanded. 30–49 year olds are among the fastest-growing segments. Gen Z, Millennials, and increasingly Gen X are all active.
1.3
TikTok as a search engine40% of Gen Z uses TikTok as their primary search tool for product discovery. This reframes how you plan content — every video is potentially searchable.
1.4
Setting up your Business Account correctlyProfile keywords in your bio, category selection, link placement, and why profile picture consistency matters for brand recall.
1.5
Understanding TikTok's subcultures#SkincareTok, #BeautyTok, #WellnessTok, #BookTok each have their own aesthetics, norms, and trust hierarchies. Violate them and you'll be ignored; speak their language and you belong.

Brand Example

Duolingo

Duolingo's TikTok success came entirely from understanding platform culture first. Rather than promoting its app, it leaned into the absurdist humor of TikTok using its owl mascot — chaotic, self-aware, funny. It never looked like a brand post. It looked like TikTok. The result was millions of followers with almost zero paid media.

Strategy

Cracking the Algorithm

The algorithm doesn't care who you are — it cares how people respond to your content. Watch time, saves, shares, and completion rate are the signals. Understand these and you understand everything.

Lessons

2.1
How the staged content rollout worksEvery video is first shown to a small test audience of ~200–500 people. Performance here determines whether it's pushed to 5K, then 50K, then beyond. Your first 200 views are a trial run.
2.2
The five ranking signals — ranked by weightWatch time and completion rate carry the most weight. Shares and saves are high-intent signals TikTok trusts. Likes and comments are secondary. Follows barely register.
2.3
Micro-virality: the 2025 community-first shiftTikTok now distributes within niche communities first (#SkincareTok, #WellnessTok) before breaking out. Going deep in a niche beats chasing mass virality.
2.4
Posting consistency and account trust scoresRegular posting trains the algorithm to treat you as reliable. Accounts that disappear for weeks are demoted. Minimum: 3–4 posts per week to maintain distribution momentum.
2.5
What silently suppresses your reachReposting content with other platforms' watermarks. Extremely low completion rate. Policy violations. Using trending sounds incorrectly flagged as copyrighted.

Creation

Content Strategy & Creation

TikTok users scroll at roughly 300ms per video. Your hook is everything. Then: pacing, format, and the right balance of authenticity and strategy.

Lessons

3.1
The hook: your most important two secondsStarts in the first frame — not after a logo intro. Use a visual surprise, a bold claim, a provocative question, or unresolved tension. The hook is not the intro, it is the content.
3.2
Video length: when short wins, when long winsUnder 30 seconds: maximum rewatches, highest completion rate. 60–90s: tutorials and before/after. 3+ minutes: storytelling and deep dives — only if every second earns its place.
3.3
Formats that consistently performPOV, Get Ready With Me (GRWM), tutorial, before/after transformation, day-in-the-life, product unboxing, and trend participation. Each has its own rules — learn them before you use them.
3.4
Authenticity vs. polishUGC-style content gets 2.4× more engagement than professionally produced brand content on TikTok. "Real" beats "refined." But real still requires strategy — you're engineering spontaneity.
3.5
Building a 5-pillar content frameworkPlan across five repeating themes: Education, Entertainment, Social Proof, Behind-the-Scenes, and Product. Rotate between them so your feed never goes stale and the algorithm sees variety.
3.6
Trend timing: how to jump in without looking lateA trend on day 3 is opportunity. On day 10 it's desperation. If you have to explain the trend to a colleague, it's over. Keep a daily 10-minute TikTok browse on your schedule.

Hook Type 1 — Claim

"This $12 product replaced my entire skincare routine."

Opens with a bold, specific, slightly unbelievable statement. Forces the viewer to stay and find out if it's true.

Hook Type 2 — Question

"Why does everyone keep buying this after seeing one video?"

Creates a social proof + curiosity gap. The viewer is already part of the story before the video starts.

Hook Type 3 — Visual

Close-up of texture, colour payoff, or transformation — zero words.

The first frame does all the work. Works best for beauty, food, and product reveals. No caption needed in the first second.

Brand Example

e.l.f. Cosmetics

e.l.f. commissioned an original song — "Eyes. Lips. Face." — specifically for TikTok and launched a Branded Hashtag Challenge (#eyeslipsface). It became one of the most viral brand moments in TikTok history because it felt native: original audio, an open invitation for participation, and no hard sell. Users made over 5 million videos using the sound.

SEO

TikTok SEO & Discovery

TikTok's search bar drives a growing share of content discovery, especially for product research and tutorials. Most brands still treat TikTok as a broadcast channel. Optimising for search is an underused advantage.

Lessons

4.1
How TikTok's search algorithm ranks contentTikTok combines relevance signals: caption keywords (highest weight), on-screen text (OCR-scanned), spoken audio, hashtags, and engagement velocity on the video.
4.2
Keyword research for TikTokUse TikTok's own search autocomplete bar, the Creative Center Keyword Insights tool, and competitor caption analysis. Find what your audience actually types, not what you think they search.
4.3
Writing captions that rankTikTok allows 2,200 characters. Use the first 150 for your primary keyword naturally placed. Add 2–3 supporting terms in the body. Never keyword-stuff — it reads as spam and hurts engagement.
4.4
Hashtag strategy: 3–5 specific tags, not 20 generic onesOne niche community tag (#SkincareTok), one mid-tier specific tag (#HydrationTips), one broader trend tag. Avoid #fyp and #foryou — they add zero ranking signal.
4.5
On-screen text, voiceover, and audio as layered SEO signalsTikTok's OCR reads every text overlay. State your primary keyword aloud in the first five seconds. These compounding signals dramatically improve search discoverability.

Brand Example

Rhode (Hailey Bieber's brand)

Rhode's "peptide lip treatment" videos dominated TikTok search because every video — caption, on-screen text, and voiceover — naturally included the exact phrase people were already searching. They didn't just make content about the product; they optimised every content layer so the product appeared every time someone searched "peptide lip" or "glazed donut skin."

Partnerships

Creator Partnerships

34% of TikTok users have purchased a product based on a creator's recommendation. The gap between a deal that converts and one that wastes budget is creator selection, brief quality, and creative freedom.

Lessons

5.1
The TikTok Creator Marketplace: how to use itTikTok's official platform for brand-creator matching. Filter by audience demographics, engagement rate, content category, and estimated reach. Free to access with a Business Account.
5.2
Creator tiers: what to expect from eachNano (1K–10K): highest engagement %, lowest cost, perfect for niche seeding. Micro (10K–100K): niche authority and trust. Macro (100K+): reach at lower engagement ratio. Choose by goal, not follower count.
5.3
Writing a creator brief that actually gets usedOne page maximum. State: the goal, one key product claim, what not to say, deliverables and timeline. Then stop. Over-scripted briefs kill authenticity — and authenticity is why you hired a creator.
5.4
Long-term partnerships vs. one-off postsAudiences notice one-off sponsorships immediately. Repeated appearances by the same creator build credibility over time. Budget for at least 3 posts before evaluating performance of any partnership.
5.5
Gifting, seeding, and affiliate structuresProduct gifting = organic UGC with no guaranteed post (but sometimes the best content). Affiliate commission = creator earns when you earn — aligns incentives perfectly at any budget level.

Brand Example

Gymshark

Gymshark built one of the most successful creator programmes in fitness by treating creators as long-term brand athletes, not transactional post-providers. They gave creators product, a brand story, and near-total creative freedom — no scripts, no hard sells. Creators could post what felt authentic to their community. The result was years of sustained growth driven almost entirely by creator content that didn't look like advertising.

Commerce

TikTok Shop & Commerce

TikTok Shop generated over $20 billion GMV in 2024 with projections to triple by 2025 year-end. Discovery → purchase without leaving the app. Conversion rates run 3–5× higher than redirect-based links.

Lessons

6.1
How TikTok Shop's native checkout worksIn-app discovery → in-app purchase → in-app tracking. No redirect, no friction, no abandoned cart from a slow external page. TikTok handles checkout, payment, and order confirmation.
6.2
Setting up your seller accountRequirements: registered business entity, government ID, bank account, and product inventory. Approval takes 1–3 days. Commission is ~8% plus a small transaction fee — lower than Amazon's 15%.
6.3
What products sell on TikTok ShopStrong visual appeal. Clear problem → solution story. Before/after potential. Under $50 for impulse buys. Beauty, skincare, gadgets, and lifestyle items consistently top category charts.
6.4
Live Shopping: the highest-converting formatReal-time demos, Q&A, and urgency-based offers (limited quantities, flash discounts) during live streams drive the platform's highest purchase intent. Minimum viable live: 30 minutes, scheduled weekly.
6.5
The 50–80 UGC video ruleThe most successful TikTok Shop products are supported by 50–80 different pieces of UGC showing the product in use. Volume ensures that statistically, something breaks through to the FYP.
6.6
TikTok Shop Affiliate ProgrammeOpen your product to any creator to promote on commission. Zero upfront cost, creator earns only when you sell. This is the fastest way to generate UGC volume at scale.

Brand Example

TIRTIR (Korean Beauty Brand)

TIRTIR's Mask Fit Red Cushion foundation went viral on TikTok Shop entirely through creator affiliate content. One creator review hit 2M views and sold out the product within hours. TIRTIR had enrolled in the TikTok Shop affiliate programme with zero upfront spend — they simply sent samples to creators who opted in and let the content do the work. They then used the viral video as a Spark Ad to extend reach further.

Paid

Paid Advertising on TikTok

On TikTok, creative is the targeting. The same audience with weak creative will fail. The same creative with broad targeting will often win. The ad format and creative strategy are inseparable.

Lessons

7.1
TikTok ad formats: which one fits which objectiveIn-Feed Ads (most common, lowest CPM). TopView (high-impact, highest cost). Branded Hashtag Challenges (community participation). Spark Ads (boosting existing content). Shopping Ads (catalogue-driven).
7.2
Spark Ads: the highest-performing format in 2025Boost organic posts — your own or a creator's — as ads. They keep the comment section, social proof, and native feel. Spark Ads outperform standard In-Feed Ads on almost every KPI. Start here.
7.3
Creative best practices: ads that don't look like adsNo logo in first 3 seconds. Open with your strongest visual. Use TikTok's native fonts and trending (non-copyrighted) sounds. Caption everything. 15–30 seconds is the sweet spot for performance.
7.4
Targeting: start broad, then narrowTikTok's interest targeting is less granular than Meta's — but strong creative finds its audience without tight targeting. Start broad. Narrow only after 1,000+ conversions in the learning phase.
7.5
Smart+ Catalogue Ads and automated campaignsFor product catalogues: TikTok's Smart+ automatically serves the most relevant product to each user based on purchase intent signals. Best for scaling past initial traction at $200+/day budgets.
7.6
Budget, learning phase, and what to expectMinimum campaign budget: $50/day. Expect a 7–14 day learning phase before optimisation stabilises. Do not make major changes (creative, targeting, budget) during the learning phase — you restart it.

Brand Example

Chipotle

Chipotle ran one of the earliest brand Spark Ad strategies by identifying organic employee videos that were performing well and boosting them — rather than producing expensive brand ads. The videos felt completely native because they were native. When one employee's "behind the scenes at Chipotle" video got traction organically, Chipotle amplified it as a Spark Ad and it became one of their highest-converting campaigns of the year.

Analytics

Analytics & Measurement

Likes are a vanity metric. Completion rate, saves per view, and profile visits driven by content are the signals that predict whether your brand is actually landing — or just being scrolled past.

Lessons

8.1
The metrics that actually predict successAverage watch time, completion rate (target: 40%+), shares per view, saves per view, and profile visits driven by a specific post. These indicate real resonance — not passive scrolling.
8.2
Reading your audience analytics for optimal posting timeIgnore generic "best time to post" guides. Open TikTok Analytics → Followers tab → Activity. Your specific audience's active hours are the only hours that matter for your account.
8.3
The monthly content audit processRank your last 30 videos by completion rate. Identify the top 25%. Find what the hook, format, topic, and length had in common. Then make more of that. This is your only data-driven brief.
8.4
Connecting TikTok content to revenueInstall TikTok Pixel on your website. Use UTM parameters on all bio links. TikTok's attribution window: 7-day click, 1-day view. For TikTok Shop, use the native Seller Center dashboard — attribution is automatic.
8.5
2025 performance benchmarksAverage engagement rate: 2.5–5%. Good completion rate: 40%+. Strong VTR for ads: 15%+. Profile visit rate from a post: 2–4% signals high audience pull. Use these to calibrate, not to obsess.

What Good Looks Like

Benchmark Reference

A skincare brand posting 4× per week should aim for: an average completion rate above 38% across all videos, at least one video per month driving 500+ profile visits, and a saves-to-views ratio of at least 1.5% on tutorial content. These aren't industry averages — they're the internal targets of brands that consistently grow on TikTok without paid support.

Community

Community & Engagement

Follower count is a lagging indicator. Community depth — the conversations your content sparks, the duets people make, the comments that write themselves — is what separates brands with longevity from one-hit wonders.

Lessons

9.1
Reply to comments — especially with videoTikTok's "reply with video" feature turns a comment into a new piece of content addressed directly to that viewer. It drives engagement loops and proves to the algorithm that your content generates real conversation.
9.2
Duets and Stitches: how to use bothDuets: react side-by-side to community content or creator posts. Stitches: clip + respond to a specific moment in another video. Use both to participate in trends and generate community dialogue.
9.3
Building a recognisable brand voiceChoose 2–3 personality traits (e.g., warm + knowledgeable + slightly self-aware). Apply them consistently in captions, comment replies, and video dialogue. Consistency builds familiarity. Familiarity builds trust.
9.4
Using your comment section as content researchPin thoughtful questions that spark debate. Comments like "wait, does this work for…?" are content briefs. Turn the most-asked questions into your next video. Your community is writing your content calendar.
9.5
TikTok LIVE for community accelerationRegular LIVEs (even 20–30 minutes, weekly) dramatically accelerate audience loyalty. Lives are boosted by the algorithm separately from regular posts and consistently pull new followers at low cost.

Brand Example

Ryanair

Ryanair's TikTok became a case study in community engagement because it leaned entirely into its reputation — self-deprecating, chaotic, and completely unbothered by being the butt of its own jokes. Every comment was an opportunity for a witty reply. Every complaint became content. The brand voice was so consistent and so native to TikTok culture that the comment section became the entertainment — and people followed just to read the replies.

Action Plan

Your 30-Day Launch Plan

Strategy means nothing until it hits publish. Week-by-week framework to launch, test, build, and scale — starting from zero.

Week by Week

W1
Infrastructure & Research (Days 1–7) Set up Business Account + TikTok Pixel. Audit 10 competitors. Define 3–5 content pillars. Identify 5–10 micro-creators for outreach. Set up TikTok Shop if you sell products. Install the Creator Marketplace.
W2
First Content Sprint (Days 8–14) Post once daily. Lead with your strongest hook-based content. Test at least two formats (e.g., tutorial vs. POV). Reply to every comment within 4 hours. Do not touch analytics yet — let it breathe.
W3
Review, Iterate & Creator Activation (Days 15–21) Run your first content audit — rank by completion rate. Double down on the top-performing format. Send gifting packages to 5–10 micro-creators. Post your first LIVE (even 20 minutes). Run one Spark Ad on your best post ($20/day test).
W4
Scale What's Working (Days 22–30) Increase to 1–2 posts daily using proven formats. Launch a low-budget ($50/day) In-Feed or Spark Ad campaign. Begin second creator wave. Set your monthly analytics review cadence. Plan Month 2 based on real data, not assumptions.
✓
Non-negotiable rules for sustained growth Never repost watermarked content from other platforms. Never buy followers. Never go dark for 3+ days. Never change a campaign during its learning phase. Never optimise for likes. Never post without a hook.
