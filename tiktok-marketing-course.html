Cracking the Algorithm

What TikTok actually rewards — and how to work with it

The algorithm does not care who you are — it cares how people react to your content. Watch time, saves, shares, replays, and completion rate are the signals that drive distribution. Understand these and you understand everything.

Strategy ~60 min read
02

Lessons in this module

  • 2.1
    How the For You Page ranking system works The algorithm tests every video through a staged rollout to progressively larger audiences. Learn the sequence and why your first 200 views define your reach.
  • 2.2
    The five ranking signals that matter most in 2025 Watch time, completion rate, shares, saves, and replays. Likes and follows have been deprioritised — deep engagement now determines reach.
  • 2.3
    Micro-virality and community-first distribution The 2025 update shifted TikTok toward community clusters (#SkincareTok, #WellnessTok) rather than mass virality. Going deep in a niche beats going broad.
  • 2.4
    Posting consistency and account trust TikTok builds a trust score for each account over time. Regular posting trains the algorithm to treat your uploads as reliable bets — meaning better baseline reach.
  • 2.5
    What kills reach: shadow signals to avoid Reposted content, heavy watermarks from other platforms, unusually low watch time, and policy violations all suppress distribution silently.

Content Strategy & Creation

Making content that earns attention — and keeps it

TikTok users are ruthless scrollers. You have under two seconds to earn a watch. Content strategy here isn't about a content calendar — it's about understanding hooks, pacing, storytelling, and the specific formats that consistently perform on the platform.

Creation ~75 min read
03

Lessons in this module

  • 3.1
    The hook: your most important two seconds A strong hook is a visual surprise, a bold claim, a direct question, or an unresolved tension. It must appear in the first frame — not after a logo intro.
  • 3.2
    Video length strategy: when short wins, when long wins Under 30 seconds drives rewatches and shares. 60–90 seconds works for tutorials and storytelling. 3+ minutes is viable if every second earns its place.
  • 3.3
    Content formats that consistently perform POV videos, "Get Ready With Me," tutorials, before/after transformations, reaction content, day-in-the-life, and trend hijacks. Know each format's rules.
  • 3.4
    Authenticity vs. polish: finding the right balance UGC-style content receives 2.4x more engagement than traditionally produced brand content on TikTok. "Real" beats "refined" — but "real" still requires strategy.
  • 3.5
    Building a content pillar framework for your brand Plan across three to five repeating content pillars (e.g., education, entertainment, social proof, behind-the-scenes) so you never run out of ideas.
  • 3.6
    Trends: how to jump in without looking desperate Timeliness is everything. A trend joined on day three is different from day ten. The rule: if you have to explain the trend to a colleague, it's already over.

Module 04 / 10

TikTok SEO & Discovery

Getting found by people who are actively looking

TikTok's search bar now drives a meaningful share of content discovery, especially for product research and tutorials. Optimising for search is an underused advantage — most brands still treat TikTok as a broadcast channel, not a searchable one.

SEO ~50 min read
04

Lessons in this module

  • 4.1
    How TikTok's search algorithm differs from Google TikTok ranks content based on a combination of relevance signals: caption keywords, on-screen text (OCR-scanned), spoken audio, hashtags, and engagement velocity.
  • 4.2
    Keyword research for TikTok: finding what your audience types Use TikTok's search autocomplete, the Creative Center's Keyword Insights tool, and competitor caption analysis to find high-intent terms in your niche.
  • 4.3
    Writing captions that rank: the 2,000-character opportunity TikTok now allows 2,200 characters. Use the first 150 for your primary keyword naturally. Weave in 2–3 supporting terms without keyword stuffing.
  • 4.4
    Hashtag strategy: quality over quantity 3–5 specific, intentional hashtags outperform 20 generic ones. Use one niche community tag, one mid-tier tag, and one broader trend tag. Avoid #fyp and #foryou — they add no signal.
  • 4.5
    On-screen text, voiceover, and audio as SEO signals TikTok's OCR reads your text overlays. State your primary keyword in the first five seconds of audio. These layered signals compound your discoverability.

Creator Partnerships

Working with influencers the right way — and the wrong way

34% of TikTok users have purchased a product based on a creator's recommendation. But the difference between a creator deal that converts and one that wastes budget comes down to creator selection, briefing quality, and how much creative freedom you give.

Partnerships ~55 min read
05

Lessons in this module

  • 5.1
    The TikTok Creator Marketplace: how it works TikTok's official platform for brand–creator connections. Access audience demographics, engagement data, and estimated reach before you reach out. Free to access.
  • 5.2
    Creator tiers and what to expect from each Nano (1K–10K): highest engagement rates, low cost. Micro (10K–100K): niche authority, great for product categories. Macro (100K+): reach but lower engagement ratio. Choose based on goal, not vanity metrics.
  • 5.3
    How to write a creator brief that actually gets used Give the objective and key message — then stop. Over-scripted briefs kill authenticity. The brief should be one page: goal, key claim, what not to say, and deliverables.
  • 5.4
    Long-term partnerships vs. one-off posts Audiences can smell a one-off sponsorship. Repeated appearances by the same creator build trust and dramatically improve conversion. Budget for at least three posts before judging performance.
  • 5.5
    Gifting, seeding, and affiliate commissions as alternatives to flat fees Product gifting generates organic UGC with no guaranteed post. Affiliate structures align incentives — creators earn when you earn. Both scale further than flat-fee deals at smaller budgets.

TikTok Shop & Commerce

Turning views into purchases — without leaving the app

TikTok Shop generated over $20 billion in GMV in 2024, with projections to triple by end of 2025. It has fundamentally changed how impulse purchases happen online. If you sell a physical product, this module is non-negotiable.

Commerce ~65 min read
06

Lessons in this module

  • 6.1
    What TikTok Shop is and how the native checkout works In-app discovery → in-app purchase. No redirect, no friction. TikTok handles checkout, payment, and order management. Conversion rates run 3–5x higher than redirect-based links.
  • 6.2
    Setting up your seller account: requirements and process You need a registered business entity, government ID, bank account, and product inventory. Setup takes 1–3 days for approval. Commission is approximately 8% + transaction fees.
  • 6.3
    Product selection: what sells on TikTok Shop Visual appeal, clear problem-solution, before/after potential, price point under $50 for impulse, and strong demonstration value. Beauty, skincare, gadgets, and lifestyle consistently top the charts.
  • 6.4
    Live Shopping: the highest-converting format on the platform Real-time product demos, Q&A, and urgency-driven offers during live streams drive the platform's highest purchase intent. Plan for a minimum 30-minute live at peak hours for your audience.
  • 6.5
    UGC content volume: the 50–80 video rule The most successful TikTok Shop products are backed by 50–80 different pieces of UGC showing the product in use. This volume ensures something breaks through to the FYP at scale.
  • 6.6
    Affiliate programmes through TikTok Shop Open your product to any creator to promote on affiliate commission. Zero upfront cost. This is the fastest way to generate volume UGC and tap audiences you can't reach organically.

Module 07 / 10

Paid Advertising on TikTok

When and how to put budget behind your content

TikTok Ads work differently from Meta or Google because the creative is everything. The same audience targeting with weak creative will fail. The same creative with different targeting will succeed. Understanding TikTok's ad formats and creative requirements is the prerequisite.

Paid ~70 min read
07

Lessons in this module

  • 7.1
    TikTok Ad formats: which one fits your objective In-Feed Ads (most common), TopView (high-impact, expensive), Branded Hashtag Challenges, Spark Ads (boosting existing organic content), and Shopping Ads. Each has a different cost and function.
  • 7.2
    Spark Ads: the highest-performing ad format in 2025 Boost organic posts — yours or a creator's — as ads. They retain the comment section, social proof, and native feel. Spark Ads outperform standard In-Feed Ads on almost every metric.
  • 7.3
    Creative best practices: making ads that don't feel like ads No logo in the first three seconds. Open with your strongest visual or claim. Use native fonts and TikTok sounds. Caption everything. Keep it under 30 seconds for top performance.
  • 7.4
    Targeting strategy: interest, behaviour, and lookalike audiences TikTok's targeting is less granular than Meta's but improves rapidly with the pixel. Start broad with strong creative and let the algorithm find your audience. Narrow only after 1,000+ conversions.
  • 7.5
    Smart+ Catalogue Ads and automated shopping campaigns For product catalogues, TikTok's Smart+ automatically serves the most relevant product to each user based on browsing and purchase signals. Best for scaling past initial traction.
  • 7.6
    Budget benchmarks and what to expect at different spend levels Minimum campaign budget is $50/day. Expect a 7–14 day learning phase before optimisation stabilises. Do not make major changes during the learning phase.

Module 08 / 10

Analytics & Measurement

What to track, what to ignore, and how to iterate

Likes are a vanity metric on TikTok. The signals that actually predict success — watch time, retention rate, saves, shares, and profile visits from content — are in your TikTok Analytics dashboard, and most brands aren't looking at the right numbers.

Analytics ~45 min read
08

Lessons in this module

  • 8.1
    The metrics that predict content success Average watch time, completion rate (aim for 40%+), shares per view, saves per view, and profile visits driven by a specific post. These indicate real resonance.
  • 8.2
    Reading your audience analytics: timing, demographics, and geography Post when your audience is active, not when social media guides tell you to. Check your own TikTok Analytics > Followers tab for your specific audience's activity windows.
  • 8.3
    The content audit process: what worked and why Monthly audit: rank your last 30 videos by completion rate. Find the top 25% and reverse-engineer what the hook, format, topic, and length had in common. Do more of that.
  • 8.4
    Attribution for TikTok: connecting content to revenue TikTok's attribution window is typically 7-day click, 1-day view. Use UTM parameters on links, TikTok Pixel for web tracking, and the TikTok Shop dashboard for in-app attribution.
  • 8.5
    Benchmarks: what good looks like in 2025 Average engagement rate: 2.5–5%. Good completion rate: 40%+. Strong VTR (view-through rate) for ads: 15%+. Profile visit rate from a post: 2–4% indicates strong audience pull.

Module 09 / 10

Community & Engagement

Building an audience that comes back and brings friends

Follower count is a lagging indicator. Community depth — the comment section, duets, stitches, and the conversations your content sparks — is what separates brands with longevity from those with a viral moment and nothing after.

Community ~40 min read
09

Lessons in this module

  • 9.1
    Reply to comments — and reply with video TikTok's "reply with video" feature turns a comment into a new piece of content addressed directly to that viewer. This drives engagement loops and shows the algorithm that your content generates real conversation.
  • 9.2
    Duets and Stitches: the TikTok collaboration formats Duets allow you to react to or add to someone else's video side-by-side. Stitches let you clip and respond to another video. Use both to participate in trends and engage with your community's content.
  • 9.3
    Building a recognisable brand voice on TikTok Pick two or three personality traits for how your brand sounds in comments, captions, and replies. Consistency builds familiarity. Familiarity builds trust. Trust drives purchase.
  • 9.4
    Leveraging your comment section as content fuel Pin thoughtful questions. Respond to criticism gracefully — the community is watching how you handle it. Screenshot and repost comments that validate your product's value.
  • 9.5
    TikTok LIVE for community building Regular LIVE sessions (even 20–30 minutes) dramatically accelerate audience loyalty. Lives are algorithm-boosted separately from regular posts and consistently pull in new followers.

Module 10 / 10

Your 30-Day Launch Plan

Putting it all together into a sequence that builds momentum

Strategy means nothing until it hits a publish button. This final module gives you a week-by-week framework to launch your TikTok presence, test what works, build an initial audience, and set up the infrastructure to scale — starting from zero.

Action Plan ~30 min read
10

Week-by-Week Framework

  • W1
    Infrastructure & Research (Days 1–7) Set up Business Account. Install TikTok Pixel. Audit 10–15 competitor accounts. Define your 3–5 content pillars. Identify 3 creators for outreach. Set up TikTok Shop if applicable.
  • W2
    First Content Sprint (Days 8–14) Post once daily. Lead with your strongest hook-based content. Test at least two formats (tutorial vs. POV vs. trend). Respond to every comment within 4 hours. Do not look at analytics yet.
  • W3
    Review, Iterate & Creator Activation (Days 15–21) Run your first content audit. Double down on the top-performing format. Send gifting packages to 5–10 micro-creators. Post your first TikTok LIVE (even 20 minutes). Begin testing one Spark Ad on your best post.
  • W4
    Scale What's Working (Days 22–30) Increase posting to 1–2x daily using proven formats. Launch a low-budget In-Feed or Spark Ad campaign. Begin a second creator wave. Set your monthly analytics review cadence. Define Month 2 goals based on real data.
  • ✓
    The non-negotiable rules: what to always keep in mind Never repost watermarked content from other platforms. Never buy followers. Never go dark for more than 3 days. Never change a campaign during its learning phase. Never optimise for likes.
